## KEY VISUAL RULES (extracted from all images)

1. **Green dominates** — grass is the canvas, everything else sits on it
2. **Warm wood brown** — buildings, fences, tools. Second most common color
3. **Blue sky** — clean and bright during day, deep blue at night
4. **Golden light** — sunrise/sunset moments create the most beautiful shots
5. **Scale matters** — character small against world = exploration feel
6. **Clusters not scatter** — trees grouped, buildings grouped, farms in rows
7. **Paths between** — dirt/stone paths connecting areas make world feel intentional
8. **Warm glow at night** — campfires, window light, lanterns = safety beacons
9. **Fog at distance** — hides edges, suggests infinite world
10. **NPCs doing things** — sitting, walking, farming = world feels alive
